Test serial numbers

Made-up serials in the same format as real stock, two for every item the app recognises by its S/N. Scan them from this screen with another phone, print the page, or tap Copy and paste them into a serial box. Drops, NIDs, mounts, poles and surge protectors have no serials, so they aren’t here.
